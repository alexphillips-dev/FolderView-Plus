### Docker privacy quick controls
- Feature: Added a compact Docker-page privacy dropdown with quick controls for names and icons, LAN IPs, ports, volume paths, image registries, public IPs, network interfaces, and external URLs.
- UI/UX: Replaced the Privacy switch's redundant ON/OFF text with a themed sliders-and-chevron dropdown button while retaining the main instant enable or disable toggle.
- Performance: Privacy option changes now apply optimistically to the current Docker page and persist immediately through the shared conflict-safe preference coordinator and retry outbox.
- UI/UX: Added dialog semantics, expanded-state reporting, outside-click dismissal, and Escape-key dismissal with focus restoration.
- Test: Expanded the privacy-mode contract guard to cover all quick options, immediate persistence wiring, removal of ON/OFF labels, and themed dropdown behavior.
